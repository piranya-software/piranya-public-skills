# Piranya MCP privacy supplement

This supplement explains the Piranya Site Management and Reporting integration provided by Piranya Software ApS, CVR 38274503. It complements our [general privacy policy](https://piranya.dk/persondatapolitik) and your applicable customer-specific service and data-processing agreements. Those agreements are provided to customers directly, not published here.

## Information processed and hosting

The connection processes identity and authorization information, installation selections, tool requests and their results to provide authorized access to your Piranya installation. Requests and results may contain website content, catalog information, business reports and personal data, depending on your task. The service also processes authorization/session records and operational/security information needed to operate and protect access.

Piranya hosts the MCP server—not your AI provider's LLM—in its Hetzner hosting environment under the applicable customer arrangements. Your AI provider hosts or arranges its own service separately; this hosting statement does not describe where that provider processes or stores information.

Where we process your organization's personal data on its behalf, the applicable data-processing agreement governs that processing. Our general privacy policy also applies where Piranya acts as controller, read together with this supplement. This supplement does not change either party's responsibilities under applicable data-protection law.

## Your chosen AI provider

You choose and obtain the AI assistant or other MCP client independently. It receives the tool requests and results involved in your workflow. Review your provider's terms, privacy information and organization settings before connecting, including its retention, training and international-transfer arrangements. Piranya does not provide that LLM service; it is not automatically Piranya's subprocessor merely because you connect it.

## Disconnection and retained information

Disconnecting a client does not undo completed changes or delete information already received by your provider. Authorization records, service logs and customer data are handled under the applicable agreements and privacy information; do not assume token expiry or client disconnection deletes all retained records.

## Contact and rights

For requests about data controlled by your organization, contact that organization. For Piranya privacy questions or help with access revocation, contact kontakt@piranya.dk. Applicable rights and complaint information are described in our general privacy policy; you may also complain to [Datatilsynet](https://www.datatilsynet.dk/).

See also our [MCP service terms](terms-of-service.md).
